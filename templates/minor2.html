🚀 Technology Adoption Prediction System

An Artificial Intelligence based prediction system that estimates whether users are likely to adopt a newly launched technology using Machine Learning and user behaviour analysis.

📖 Project Overview

Technology companies launch thousands of digital products every year, but many fail because users do not adopt them. This project predicts whether users are likely to adopt a technology within the first 30 days after launch, helping companies improve marketing strategies, customer engagement and product success.

🎯 Project Objective

Build an AI-powered prediction model that identifies potential adopters before product launch so companies can take early actions to increase adoption rates.

✨ Key Features

🤖 AI Prediction

Predicts whether users are likely to adopt new technology.

📊 User Behaviour

Analyses demographic and behavioural factors influencing adoption.

🎯 Marketing Support

Helps companies target the right audience.

📈 Adoption Analytics

Estimates adoption probability for each user.

⚡ Business Insights

Supports product strategy and business decisions.

🔒 Ethical AI

Encourages privacy, fairness and responsible AI.

🛠 Technologies Used

Python Pandas NumPy Scikit-Learn Flask HTML CSS JavaScript

🧠 AI Workflow

📥 Data Collection

Gather user, product and marketing information.

🧹 Data Processing

Clean, transform and prepare data.

🤖 Model Training

Train Machine Learning classification models.

📊 Prediction

Estimate technology adoption probability.

📈 Decision Making

Improve marketing and product strategy.
